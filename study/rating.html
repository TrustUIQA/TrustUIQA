Which underwater photo looks better?

You will see about 110 pairs of underwater photos. In each pair, both images show the same scene. Pick the one with better overall quality.

This screen is narrow. Please use a laptop or desktop if you can; small screens hide the differences we are studying.

How to rate

  1. Use a laptop or desktop screen at normal brightness. Turn off night mode or blue-light filters. Do not zoom.
  2. Click the image with better overall quality, or press ← for left and → for right.
  3. Go with your first impression. Three to five seconds per pair is enough.
  4. You must pick one. If they look the same to you, pick either.
  5. Judge image quality only, not which scene you like more.
  6. There is a short break halfway. There are no right answers.
